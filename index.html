About the Game

Red Light Challenge is an original front-end mini game inspired by classic stop-and-go survival mechanics. It is designed to be easy to understand in seconds while still feeling tense and replayable.

Because the entire experience runs directly in the browser with lightweight code, it loads quickly and works well as the first game on a growing web game site.

How to Play

Start a run, watch the signal, and move only when the board shows green. The more carefully you control your timing, the better your chance of reaching the finish line with a strong time.

  • Press Start to begin a new run.
  • Hold the move button or spacebar when the signal is green.
  • Release immediately when the signal turns red.
  • Reach the finish zone to win.

Controls

The controls are intentionally simple, making the game easy to pick up on both desktop and mobile devices.

  • Desktop: hold the spacebar to move.
  • Mobile: press and hold the on-screen move button.
  • Tap Restart any time to begin again.

Tips for Better Runs

Fast times come from controlled reactions, not panic. Stay calm, watch for the change, and release quickly when the signal turns red.

  • Do not chase speed too early.
  • Stay focused on the signal board.
  • Practice short bursts instead of holding too aggressively.
  • Replay runs to improve timing consistency.

FAQ

Yes. Your best time is stored locally in your browser using local storage.
